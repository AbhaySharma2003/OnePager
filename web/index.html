Questions

Will it make up achievements?

No. Every number, date, company and place in your one-pager has to exist in your Master CV, and OnePager checks this before you download. Where a number would make a bullet stronger, it asks you to add it.

Does it follow a one-page template exactly?

Yes. It uses a classic one-page resume grid and a strict checklist: gridlines, bold section labels, RAC bullets, Class X / XII and the score rules.

What can I upload?

A PDF or Word CV, or a Master CV Excel. If you don't have a CV, fill the Master CV form instead.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. Edit any line on the page, rewrite the whole one-pager, or download the Word file and change it yourself.

How does payment work?

Your first one-pager is free. For each one after that, you go through the whole process for free and pay ₹49 only once it's ready, to see and download it.

Who can see my CV?

Only you. Your Master CV and one-pagers are saved privately to your account.
